About the App

DV-Writing Assistant is a mobile-first writing workspace tailored to provide professional paraphrasing, grammar detection, abstract summarization, and academic bibliography management directly on Android devices.

Built with zero heavyweight JavaScript frameworks, it leverages standard Web APIs, IndexedDB storage, and high-performance client styling adhering strictly to Material Design 3 guidelines.

Why I Built This

Most writing tools and paraphrasers are designed for full desktop monitors, leaving mobile writers with cramped interfaces, disruptive ads, and unresponsive textboxes.

DV-Writing Assistant was built to solve this disparity by introducing a clean Android-tailored environment where researchers, authors, and students can draft, paraphrase, check grammar, and generate verified citations natively on touch devices.

How to Use

1. Write or Paste

Type into the primary editor or press the Paste button to quickly ingest text from your Android clipboard.

2. Choose an Action Tile

Tap Paraphraser to generate 3 diverse sentence forms, Summarizer to distill core premises, or Grammar Checker to identify inline syntax mistakes.

3. Citations & Bibliography

Use the Citation Gen tile to look up papers by DOI or books by title, then format them straight into APA or MLA style.
